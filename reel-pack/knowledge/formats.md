# Formats at a glance

| Format | Skill | Length | Hook | Body | Payoff | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mechanism reveal | `mechanism-reveal` | 30-60 s | "How does X do Y without Z?" | 4-6 mechanism beats, machine as subject | the whole machine working | machines, inventions, nature |
| Build-Up | `build-up-script` | 60-90 s | one physical number, scaled up | 5-7 rungs, "you" fix each problem and cause the next | "and that is how you turn X into Y", name said last | how things evolved, strategy |
| Myth explainer | `myth-explainer` | 25-32 s | "If you..." already happening in frame 1 | about 20 one-second shots, a silent man in a blue grid studio, 1-3 word captions, the sentence breaks off on its noun | the answer with a short build-up and a callback | physics, survival, "what would really happen" |
| 3D build short | `build-short-3d` | 30-90 s | the finished thing, or the empty site | one continuous camera move, pieces assemble in order | it runs | infrastructure, machinery |
| Myth-flip photo | `myth-flip-photo` | 15-30 s | one recognisable real photo | 2-3 text beats | the true pivot | cheap, shareable, viral claims |
| Coded motion graphics | `motion-graphics-render-loop` | 15-45 s | product line or result | UI cards, springs, cuts on the beat | outcome line | product, tool and skill promos |
| Single hero shot | `scene-3d` + `hardware-hero-motion` | 4-15 s | the action itself | one camera move, one event | the hit / reveal | B-roll, demos, inserts |

## Typical reel skeleton (story reels)
1. Hook (0-2 s): question or claim + something already moving.
2. Place (optional, 2-4 s): where/when, ideally a camera drop from far to near when a location is named.
3. Beats (most of the reel): one idea per beat, one camera move per shot.
4. Proof (optional): 2-4 real licensed photos with one-line names.
5. Payoff: the slowest line, the biggest picture.
6. CTA: one action, short tail.

## Before/after demo block (for showing what a pack or tool adds)
Same prompt, two renders: left or first, the plain result; right or second, the crewed result (light, haze, camera moves, FX, sound). Keep both at least 2 s per shot, label each in the lower third, no voice over the comparison, real SFX only.
