# Build-Up script

The viewer is the clever one doing the building. That is why it holds attention: it is their problem, their fix, their next problem.

## Shape (60-90 s)
1. **Hook on one physical number, scaled up.** "You need to move a tank across a river that's forty metres wide."
2. **5-7 rungs, about 10-12 s each.** Each rung:
   - the problem, plainly;
   - the fix, as one physical action "you" take ("so you weld a thicker plate on the front");
   - the fix shown working on screen;
   - the new problem the fix causes ("but now it's too heavy for the bridge").
3. **The proper name comes last**, on its own, after the viewer has already built the thing ("...and that's the Panther.").
4. **Payoff**: "and that is how you turn <opening number/problem> into <prize>."
5. **CTA**, tied to the topic.

## Rules
- **One "you"** the whole way through, one side. Never "you're Athens... now you're Rome".
- **Each problem grows out of the last fix** wherever that is true; no problems arriving from nowhere.
- **One step at a time.** After each rung the viewer could say what countered what and why. Fewer, clearer rungs beat more.
- **A signature opener ("you're one step ahead") may appear once.** After that vary the lead-in ("so then you", "which leads to", "you decide to", "now", "but then") or use none. A repeated refrain sounds ridiculous by rung three.
- **Full plain sentences**, no clipped fragments.
- **Show it**: each fix happens on the real object (the shell hits the actual tank, not a test plate). For strategy, once or twice per reel use a clean top-down view with arrows, then zoom right in on a one-on-one that shows why the move works. Top-down views must still look like a 3D world, not a slide.
- **Real things get a small real photo inset with a one-line name** when they first appear, if you have a licensed photo.
- Copy the scripting technique only, never another creator's words or catchphrase.

## Template
```
HOOK: You need to <task> but <physical number / constraint>.
RUNG 1: So you <fix>. <shown working>. But now <new problem caused by the fix>.
RUNG 2: <lead-in> you <fix>. ... But <problem>.
...
NAME: That's the <name>.
PAYOFF: And that is how you turn <start> into <prize>.
CTA: <one action>
```
