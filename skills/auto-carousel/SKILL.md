# Auto-carousel

**The idea:** carousels made in one consistent bespoke look, from a topic pipeline, instead of hand-designing every post. One idea per slide, hook slide first. Creators running this say auto-made carousels match or beat hand-made ones on saves and shares. Don't claim that for your account until your own analytics show it.

## Method
1. **Brand lock (once, then reuse):**
   - Read your brand profile: fonts, colours, tone.
   - If there's no carousel style saved, make 2-3 sample slides, pick one, and save the spec as STYLE.md: colours, fonts, grid, accent rule, slide count.
   - Never improvise a new look per post.
2. **Topic list:** draft 5-10 topics from your content pillars, recent launches, trends and saved posts, one line each plus the hook. You approve or strike them. Track each topic's status: approved / drafting / review / scheduled / posted.
3. **Write, per approved topic, 8-10 slides:**
   - slide 1: the hook
   - slides 2-8: one point each, about 15 words max
   - final slide: a CTA (comment keyword / save / share / follow)
   - plus the caption and hashtags
4. **Render in the locked style:**
   - Use HTML-to-PNG (or Remotion stills) for anything with text, so you get exact fonts and no garbled AI text. Use AI image generation only for background textures and photos.
   - Size 1080x1350 (4:5).
   - Look at every slide and fix typos and clipping.
5. **Queue for approval:** save the PNGs and caption in a review folder for that post.
6. **On approval:** schedule it in whichever scheduler you use. A week later, pull the results and use them to choose the next topics.

## Tools
HTML-to-image or Remotion, ffmpeg, optionally an image generator for backgrounds (check credits first).

## Guardrails
- Nothing posts without your approval of that exact carousel. Approving a topic is not approving the post.
- State any credit spend estimate before generating.
- Never bake text into AI images; render text with real fonts.
- No made-up stats, results or testimonials.
