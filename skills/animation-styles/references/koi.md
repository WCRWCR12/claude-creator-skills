# Koi family

East Asian traditions: shadow puppets, woodblock prints, paper-cut and lightbox paper.

All styles here are coded (Remotion or HTML + ffmpeg). Plain-English look first, specs after.

## Koi 1

**What it looks like:** Shadow Puppetry: Ten suns scorch the earth until the archer Hou Yi draws their bow, told with leather puppets on a lit screen

- **Palette:** #210C05, #030101, #FEF7D0, #7D2E14, #52200E
- **Type:** DM Serif Display headline with Manrope body (Soft serif pairing).
- **Layout:** Full-bleed type: Type fills the frame edge to edge; card tucks below.
- **Texture:** Paper: Fibre, deckle edges, offset shadows.
- **Motion:** Word slam: Words slam in from 125% with a small tilt.
- **Best for:** Myths & folklore, Heritage, Festivals

## Koi 2

**What it looks like:** Ukiyo-e: A traveller walks toward a distant mountain; every shot is a woodblock print, a great wave at the climax, the mountain and the whole scroll at the end

- **Palette:** #425770, #BFA47D, #F3E8CA, #C3AC80, #213451
- **Type:** Fraunces headline with DM Sans body (Editorial serif pairing).
- **Layout:** Split: Headline owns the top half, the card the bottom.
- **Texture:** Ink & print: Hatched ink lines and print bleed on paper.
- **Motion:** Slide-stack: Lines rise and stack, staggered.
- **Best for:** Travel, Japanese culture, Poetry

## Koi 3

**What it looks like:** Red Paper-cut: On New Year's Eve the beast Nian comes to town, and one girl's giant paper-cut lights up the village to scare it off

- **Palette:** #373D71, #9B1223, #BCAFBF, #6D74AB, #9B1223
- **Type:** Alfa Slab One headline with Oswald body (Slab pairing).
- **Layout:** Tilted cards: Scrapbook: cards dropped at angles.
- **Texture:** Paper: Fibre, deckle edges, offset shadows.
- **Motion:** Drop & bounce: Falls from above and bounces.
- **Best for:** Lunar New Year, Festive greetings, Folk tales

## Koi 4

**What it looks like:** Paper-cut Lightbox: An old night train makes its last run, from a snowy mountain halt through a long tunnel to the sea, its lit windows going out one by one at the end of the line

- **Palette:** #011844, #001032, #F5F2EB, #3A5295, #00133A
- **Type:** DM Serif Display headline with Manrope body (Soft serif pairing).
- **Layout:** Centred card: Headline up top, one UI card centre stage.
- **Texture:** Paper: Fibre, deckle edges, offset shadows.
- **Motion:** Morph: A blob morphs into the card, type blurs in.
- **Best for:** Journeys and travel, Farewells and anniversaries, Places and their stories

## Koi 5

**What it looks like:** Archive Woodblock: Japanese woodblock prints laid on a cool backlit archive table; a loupe glides across, carved ink lines rise out of each print, catalogue tags pin to the paper

- **Palette:** #2A3747, #4A5A6E, #F3E8CA, #BFA47D, #8A8985
- **Type:** Fraunces headline with Space Mono body (Editorial serif + typewriter pairing).
- **Layout:** Split with tilted inserts: headline up top, woodblock card bottom, catalogue tags dropped at angles.
- **Texture:** Carved ink on paper plus a soft light-table grain and soft vignette; print bleed on warm paper.
- **Motion:** Slide-stack with a drop: carved ink lines rise and stack, staggered; catalogue cards fall and bounce in.
- **Best for:** Brand history on a heritage product, Japanese craft stories, Museum-style explainers, calm 'ingredients' reveals, any line where a claim should feel measured, quiet and documented.
