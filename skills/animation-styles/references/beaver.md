# Beaver family

Materials and 3D: brick toy, pop-up book, tilt-shift, low-poly, glass, dioramas.

All styles here are coded (Remotion or HTML + ffmpeg). Plain-English look first, specs after.

## Beaver 1

**What it looks like:** Brick Toy: A brick astronaut builds a rocket from spare parts and flies to a brick moon

- **Palette:** #C9C7C3, #A6A5A4, #3C3B31, #9B3A2B, #C7B496
- **Type:** Titan One headline with Fredoka body (Playful pairing).
- **Layout:** Tilted cards: Scrapbook: cards dropped at angles.
- **Texture:** Brutalist: Thick outlines, hard offset shadows.
- **Motion:** Drop & bounce: Falls from above and bounces.
- **Best for:** Toys & kids, Product builds, Space
