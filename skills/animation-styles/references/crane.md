# Crane family

Hand-drawn and painted: watercolour, oil, crayon, pen, chalk. Painted in stroke by stroke.

All styles here are coded (Remotion or HTML + ffmpeg). Plain-English look first, specs after.

## Crane 1

**What it looks like:** Crayon Picture Book: The moon can't sleep, so a little girl climbs onto the roof to sing it a lullaby

- **Palette:** #43486C, #31375E, #F5F2EB, #C9AD61, #A68959
- **Type:** Titan One headline with Fredoka body (Playful pairing).
- **Layout:** Tilted cards: Scrapbook: cards dropped at angles.
- **Texture:** Painterly wash: Soft pooled colour washes on fibrous paper.
- **Motion:** Drop & bounce: Falls from above and bounces.
- **Best for:** Picture books, Bedtime stories, Kids' learning
