# Otter family

Information films: screencasts, HUDs, transit maps, circuit boards, isometric explainers.

All styles here are coded (Remotion or HTML + ffmpeg). Plain-English look first, specs after.

## Otter 1

**What it looks like:** Living Screencast: Clawd hops out of the terminal into the Claude app and acts out plan mode and self-checks in one take

- **Palette:** #FAF9F5, #F4F3EE, #111111, #D5C3B2, #E8DECE
- **Type:** Bungee headline with JetBrains Mono body (Arcade pairing).
- **Layout:** UI frame: Everything sits inside an app window.
- **Texture:** 64-bit: Chunky pixels, scanlines, hard shadows.
- **Motion:** Slide-stack: Lines rise and stack, staggered.
- **Best for:** Product tutorials, Feature launches, Onboarding

## Hologram Presenter

**What it looks like:** Hologram Presenter: a talking-head take projected as a cyan hologram bust over a glowing disc, chest dissolving into ASCII characters

- **Palette:** #050E10, #132A2E, #4CDBFF, #E8FFFF, #FFB840
- **Type:** Space Mono HUD labels (Space Grotesk for any headline), amber for the one live number.
- **Layout:** Head-and-shoulders bust centre frame, projection disc below, HUD corner brackets and a halo ring behind the head.
- **Texture:** Neon cyan hologram: the real footage is cut out and recoloured cyan, with edge glow, wireframe lines on the face, CRT scanlines, a light cone from the disc, and the chest breaking up into monospace characters. Deeper variant: the face reads as a 3D volume (depth shading, contour lines across the face, rim light), faint ghost shells offset in depth, and a god-ray cone with drifting dust.
- **Motion:** Boots in as glyphs, a scan line sweeps down and the person resolves into the hologram; a target box locks onto the face and its number rolls from garbage symbols to 99.2%; slight flicker, two short glitch tears, a voice meter rides the real voice; flickers off at the end. Deeper variant: slow 3D sway with parallax (head moves more than shoulders) and an interference ring sweeping through the depth.
- **Best for:** An 'AI version of me' beat, intros and sign-offs, any line about AI, assistants or the future. Works on any filmed take.
