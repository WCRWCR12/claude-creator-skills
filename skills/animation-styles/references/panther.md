# Panther family

Dark keynote: black stages, glowing data, glass rows.

All styles here are coded (Remotion or HTML + ffmpeg). Plain-English look first, specs after.

## Panther 1

**What it looks like:** Black keynote counter: paper stack grows, number counts, particle burst

- **Palette:** #0A0A0A, #FFF4EA, #FF8A4C, #FFB27A
- **Type:** Huge Inter ExtraBold number, tiny tracked orange caps label, highlight-chip captions above.
- **Layout:** Caption top, one physical object centre (stack, avatar, logo), number under it.
- **Texture:** Pure black with a warm radial glow; light grain.
- **Motion:** The object builds as the number counts up, then a particle burst on the final value.
- **Best for:** Big numbers: pages, emails, dollars, hours.

## Panther 2

**What it looks like:** Black method list: sans title, italic serif line, rows light up green

- **Palette:** #050505, #FFFFFF, #27C46B, rgba(255,255,255,.06)
- **Type:** Inter Bold title plus an Instrument Serif italic second line; rows in Inter SemiBold with numbers.
- **Layout:** Title top, three glass rows, small presenter window below.
- **Texture:** Pure black, faint row borders, green glow on the active row.
- **Motion:** Rows rise in staggered; the active row turns solid green with a glow as each step is spoken.
- **Best for:** Named methods and frameworks ('The Triple Hook'), 3-step formulas.

## Panther 3

**What it looks like:** Neon line chart in a dark rounded panel

- **Palette:** #000000, #FF3B47, #2CFF7A, rgba(255,255,255,.35)
- **Type:** Inter Bold title, small line labels in the line colour.
- **Layout:** Title top, rounded black panel with axes and two curves, presenter window below.
- **Texture:** Neon glow on the lines, faint gridlines.
- **Motion:** Lines draw left to right with a dot riding the tip.
- **Best for:** Before/after trends, 'X drops when Y', retention or skip-rate points.

## Panther 4

**What it looks like:** Isometric glossy blue blocks on black

- **Palette:** #08090B, #BFE2FF, #4A97E8, #2C6CC0, #FFD07A
- **Type:** Inter Bold caption under the scene, mono labels.
- **Layout:** Black top half with a row of isometric blocks (one per item), presenter below.
- **Texture:** Glossy gradient tops, soft blue radial glow.
- **Motion:** Blocks drop in one by one; the key block lifts and changes colour.
- **Best for:** Timelines, clips/segments, 'one of these is different' beats.

## Panther 5

**What it looks like:** Isometric neon agent office with glowing status cards

- **Palette:** #0B0E14, #16C2A3, #E0457B, #3B6BFF, #E6C229
- **Type:** White label pill title; glass cards with a mono status line and a bold count.
- **Layout:** Four coloured isometric floor tiles with desks, a central hub, floating status cards.
- **Texture:** Dot grid, neon glows, dashed animated connection lines.
- **Motion:** Tiles rise in; connection dashes flow from the hub; cards pop.
- **Best for:** Agent teams, the Virtual Office, 'your business running itself'.
