# Lynx family

Graphic and type: Swiss grid, spy titles, Art Deco, blueprint, stained glass, signs, terminals.

All styles here are coded (Remotion or HTML + ffmpeg). Plain-English look first, specs after.

## Lynx 1

**What it looks like:** Swiss Motion Graphics: A concert poster lays itself out by five Swiss design rules; the fifth is to break just one

- **Palette:** #EAE9E5, #FFFFFF, #111111, #8C5D5E, #E9E9E4
- **Type:** Archivo Black headline with Inter body (Heavy grotesk pairing).
- **Layout:** Full-bleed type: Type fills the frame edge to edge; card tucks below.
- **Texture:** Flat: Clean colour, nothing on top.
- **Motion:** Word slam: Words slam in from 125% with a small tilt.
- **Best for:** Brand identity, Events, Design education

## Lynx 2

- **Palette:** #1B1714, #191613, #CA6148, #C83820, #C83921
- **Type:** Righteous headline with Rubik body (Retro pairing).
- **Layout:** Full-bleed type: Type fills the frame edge to edge; card tucks below.

## Lynx 4

**What it looks like:** Blueprint: An inventor's blueprint draws, explodes and assembles a cloud-catching machine, and its revision cloud starts to rain

- **Palette:** #1B3A75, #1A386F, #F5F2EB, #7589AC, #1D3D78
- **Type:** Space Grotesk headline with Space Mono body (Tech grotesk pairing).
- **Layout:** UI frame: Everything sits inside an app window.
- **Texture:** Grid paper: Fine grid lines.
- **Motion:** Slide-stack: Lines rise and stack, staggered.
- **Best for:** Engineering, Hardware launches, Tech explainers

## Lynx 5

**What it looks like:** Stained Glass: Sunlight crosses a cathedral window from dawn to dusk, waking each pane of a knight-and-dragon tale

- **Palette:** #0E0E0E, #090909, #F5F2EB, #5A5B64, #0A0B0D
- **Type:** DM Serif Display headline with Manrope body (Soft serif pairing).
