# Heron family

Real screens and documents: highlight the thing on the actual page.

All styles here are coded (Remotion or HTML + ffmpeg). Plain-English look first, specs after.

## Heron 1

**What it looks like:** Zoomed real document with a blue highlight sweep

- **Palette:** #FDFDFC, #222222, #3884FF, #1A1C20
- **Type:** The document's own type, very large; white bold caption with a dark shadow.
- **Layout:** Document fills the frame at an angle, zoomed so 4-6 lines are visible; caption in the lower third.
- **Texture:** Slight perspective, vignette, rack-focus blur at the start.
- **Motion:** Slow push; a blue highlighter sweeps across the key phrase as it is spoken.
- **Best for:** Quoting research, Reddit or newsletter findings, any 'this study found' line.

## Heron 2

**What it looks like:** Talking head over the live web page, highlighter on the line

- **Palette:** #FFFFFF, #FFE600, #000000
- **Type:** One-word white captions with a thick black outline; logos big when named.
- **Layout:** The real web page fills the frame; presenter cut-out (or an arch) at the bottom.
- **Texture:** Real page screenshot, multiply-blend yellow highlight.
- **Motion:** Page scrolls slowly; the highlight wipes across the line being read; logos punch in.
- **Best for:** Repo or site walkthroughs, 'this is the page' moments.

## Heron 3

**What it looks like:** Tilted screenshot cards over the presenter with numbered badge pops

- **Palette:** #FFFFFF, #FFD23F, #111111
- **Type:** White labels on the cards; chunky yellow numbered badges.
- **Layout:** Two rounded screenshot cards tilted top-left and top-right, badges between them, face below.
- **Texture:** White card borders, deep shadows over real footage.
- **Motion:** Cards drop in and settle at an angle; badges pop 1-2-3.
- **Best for:** My video vs theirs, before/after, numbered steps.

## Heron 4

**What it looks like:** Phone-filmed screen with a black rounded step caption

- **Palette:** #050507, #FFFFFF, rgba(10,10,10,.88)
- **Type:** Inter SemiBold step caption ('1. Do X.') in a black rounded box.
- **Layout:** Screen footage at an angle filling the frame, caption box lower-middle.
- **Texture:** Real screen with scanlines, vignette, grain: deliberately raw.
- **Motion:** Handheld drift; the caption box changes per step.
- **Best for:** Fast build tutorials, night-build vlogs.

## Heron 5

**What it looks like:** Mint fresh · Playful · Lower third · Glass · Liquid

- **Palette:** #E6F7F0, #0E2A22, #00A878, #FFB800, #FFFFFF
- **Type:** Titan One headline with Fredoka body (Playful pairing).
- **Layout:** Lower third: Card high, headline anchored bottom-left.
- **Texture:** Glass: Colour blobs behind frosted cards.
- **Motion:** Liquid: Wavy reveal, like poured paint.
- **Best for:** Studio-built style (new).

## Heron 6

**What it looks like:** Big floating UI cards and real screens over a small talking head

- **Palette:** #E9EDF1, #0F1720, #2F7BFF, #BFD7FF, #FFFFFF
- **Type:** Archivo Black headline with Inter body (Heavy grotesk pairing).
- **Layout:** Centred card: Headline up top, one UI card centre stage.
- **Texture:** Glass: Colour blobs behind frosted cards.
- **Motion:** Spring pop: Scale up on a lively spring.
- **Best for:** PRIMARY for half-generated VO reels (Post 2): VO over big UI cards + real screens, face only start/end
