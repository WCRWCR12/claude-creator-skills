# Storytelling family

Documentary storytelling: a story told one sentence per scene.

All styles here are coded (Remotion or HTML + ffmpeg). Plain-English look first, specs after.

## Storytelling

**What it looks like:** Documentary story on a periwinkle stage: B/W photo cut-outs + letter-by-letter kinetic type

- **Palette:** #D3D8FB, #9AA3F0, #5A65C7, #1E1D4C, #6B74D9, #FFFFFF
- **Type:** Keywords in heavy condensed navy caps (Anton) typed letter by letter as spoken; connectors in small light condensed italic (Oswald, slanted) tucked beside them.
- **Layout:** Text block above (y ~330) and below (y ~1380) one centred subject; one sentence per scene; one-word beats get a bare stage.
- **Texture:** Periwinkle stage, lighter centre to deeper edges (flat variant available), fine woven-canvas texture; B/W photo cut-outs with a blue cast and soft blue shadow; pixel-art accents.
- **Motion:** Hard cut per sentence; subject drifts very slowly; keywords type on letter by letter; connectors pop in whole; white orbit ring draws on; white ring pulse on impact words; pixel accents build square by square.
- **Best for:** Story-led reels: a historical/analogy hook, a turn to now, a thesis line; narrated VO where the on-screen type is the caption.
