# Rabbit family

Cartoon and anime: rubber hose, 80s cel anime, sitcom toon, mid-century cartoon.

All styles here are coded (Remotion or HTML + ffmpeg). Plain-English look first, specs after.

## Rabbit 1

**What it looks like:** 1930s Rubber Hose Cartoon: A coffee cup chases a runaway sugar cube around the kitchen, 1930s-cartoon style

- **Palette:** #000000, #1A1917, #EBEBE9, #959289, #010000
- **Type:** Titan One headline with Fredoka body (Playful pairing).
- **Layout:** Character host: A cartoon host bottom-right; headline and UI stickers above.
- **Texture:** Brutalist: Thick outlines, hard offset shadows.
- **Motion:** Spring pop: Scale up on a lively spring.
- **Best for:** Food & drink, Retro ads, Kids
