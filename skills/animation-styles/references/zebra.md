# Zebra family

Bold kinetic type: one or two words doing all the work.

All styles here are coded (Remotion or HTML + ffmpeg). Plain-English look first, specs after.

## Zebra 1

**What it looks like:** One blood-red serif word over a dark moody portrait

- **Palette:** #07080A, #C8101E, #3C0000
- **Type:** One word at a time, heavy high-contrast serif caps (Playfair Black), ~200 px, red with a dark drop shadow.
- **Layout:** Word centred in the top third, face full-frame below it; nothing else on screen.
- **Texture:** Crushed, desaturated portrait grade, vignette, grain.
- **Motion:** Word swaps on every stressed word, each one scaling down from 118% on a hard spring. Quick cut-aways to wide shots between.
- **Best for:** Hooks and punchy statement lines; any line with 3-6 stressed words.

## Zebra 2

**What it looks like:** Giant orange lowercase punch-word over the face

- **Palette:** #000000, #FF5A1F, #FFFFFF
- **Type:** Single lowercase word, Inter ExtraBold ~190 px, tight negative tracking, orange.
- **Layout:** Word sits at chest height over the full-frame face (or over a black frame).
- **Texture:** Clean, no grain; slight bottom darkening.
- **Motion:** Hard pop: scale 70% to 100% on a stiff spring, gone on the next beat. Pairs with the white-grid data cards of Owl 2.
- **Best for:** The one key term in a sentence (a product name, a verb like 'booked', a jargon word).

## Zebra 3

**What it looks like:** One heavy white CAPS word over a striking real visual

- **Palette:** #FFFFFF, #000000
- **Type:** Archivo Black / heavy grotesk caps, ~120 px, white with a hard black shadow. One word per beat.
- **Layout:** Word low-centre over a full-bleed real photo or clip that literally shows the metaphor.
- **Texture:** Whatever the footage has; slight bottom gradient for legibility.
- **Motion:** Slow push-in on the visual; words slam in from 125% scale on each stressed word.
- **Best for:** Visual-metaphor hooks ('when X happens... it's actually Y'), myth-busting openers.

## Zebra 4

**What it looks like:** Glowing green comment-word call to action

- **Palette:** #C6FF7A, #2CFF3C, #FFFFFF
- **Type:** Small italic serif 'comment' above a huge quoted keyword in ExtraBold sans with a neon green glow.
- **Layout:** Lower third over a close-up of the face on the last line.
- **Texture:** Soft bloom glow that breathes.
- **Motion:** Keyword springs up from 60%; the glow pulses gently while the face finishes the line.
- **Best for:** End-of-reel comment-to-get CTA.

## Zebra 5

**What it looks like:** Keynote captions with a pale-blue highlight chip on the live word

- **Palette:** #0B0B0C, #F2F2F2, #8CB4FF
- **Type:** Medium-weight sans, 2-3 short lines, centred; the spoken word gets a rounded pale-blue selection chip, unspoken words dimmed.
- **Layout:** Captions near the top on a pure black or off-white stage, one small product object below.
- **Texture:** None: flat black/white, Apple-keynote clean.
- **Motion:** Chip jumps word to word in sync with speech; the stage flips black/white on section changes; objects spring in under the text.
- **Best for:** The caption system for any reel; voice-over-only explainers.

## Zebra 6

**What it looks like:** Stacked ticker bands of repeating caps sliding in opposite directions

- **Palette:** #FFD60A, #FF3B30, #0A84FF, #111111, #F2F2F2
- **Type:** Anton / condensed caps, ~96 px, a repeated phrase with bullet separators.
- **Layout:** 6-7 full-width bands stacked and tilted about 7 degrees in the top half; presenter below.
- **Texture:** Flat saturated colour blocks.
- **Motion:** Bands fly in alternately from left and right, then scroll continuously in opposite directions.
- **Best for:** Section breaks, 'here's how it works' transitions, title beats.

## Zebra 7

**What it looks like:** Data orange · Condensed news · Ticker bands · Brutalist · Calm drift

- **Palette:** #FAFAF8, #141414, #FF5A1F, #F7A27F, #FFFFFF
- **Type:** Bebas Neue headline in caps with Libre Franklin body (Condensed news pairing).
- **Layout:** Ticker bands: Repeating bands slide in opposite directions.
- **Texture:** Brutalist: Thick outlines, hard offset shadows.
- **Motion:** Calm drift: Fade and float, slow and premium.
- **Best for:** Studio-built style (new).

## Zebra 8

**What it looks like:** Sepia parchment · Futurist · Ticker bands · Flat · Flip

- **Palette:** #E9DDC0, #2F2416, #7A3B22, #B08A55, #F4ECD8
- **Type:** Unbounded headline in caps with Inter body (Futurist pairing).
- **Layout:** Ticker bands: Repeating bands slide in opposite directions.
- **Texture:** Flat: Clean colour, nothing on top.
- **Motion:** Flip: Cards flip in on their axis.
- **Best for:** Studio-built style (new).

## Zebra 9

**What it looks like:** Seaside · Poster · Full-bleed type · Glass · Mask wipe

- **Palette:** #F3EADB, #1D3A4A, #D63A2F, #FFD23F, #FFFDF7
- **Type:** Anton headline in caps with Inter body (Poster pairing).
- **Layout:** Full-bleed type: Type fills the frame edge to edge; card tucks below.
- **Texture:** Glass: Colour blobs behind frosted cards.
- **Motion:** Mask wipe: Hard-edged wipes reveal each piece.
- **Best for:** Studio-built style (new).
