# Seal family

Paper, collage and craft: tactile, textured, hand-made looks.

All styles here are coded (Remotion or HTML + ffmpeg). Plain-English look first, specs after.

## Seal 1

**What it looks like:** Cardboard paper-craft diorama with a little paper mascot

- **Palette:** #C9A15B, #8B5E3C, #F3EAD6, #D8462F, #F08A5D, #2B2620
- **Type:** Masking-tape label in heavy sans caps at the top; caption words on torn red ribbon strips in a bold serif.
- **Layout:** A little stage set: textured paper back wall, cardboard floor, props and UI cut out of paper sitting in the scene, mascot standing in it.
- **Texture:** Visible paper fibre, torn deckle edges, hard offset drop shadows, tape corners, film grain, heavy vignette.
- **Motion:** Props drop in from below on bouncy springs; mascot hops in and sways; ribbon words slap in one by one with small random tilts. Each beat swaps the whole set.
- **Best for:** Explaining a product step by step (a scene per step), price-vs-alternative hooks, open-source / tool reveals.

## Seal 2

**What it looks like:** Vintage engraving collage on old parchment

- **Palette:** #E9DDC0, #6B5537, #2F2416, #7A3B22, #4A3620
- **Type:** Small spaced caps 'PLATE IV' header, italic old-style serif subtitle, big classical serif caps headline with an italic serif second line.
- **Layout:** Centred collage: one hero cut-out in the middle, smaller cut-outs orbiting it, headline in the lower third.
- **Texture:** Parchment mottling, sepia engraving treatment with line-screen hatching, torn edges, soft vignette, fine grain.
- **Motion:** Cut-outs float up and settle; tick-ring draws on; objects drift slowly. Calm, museum pace, no hard cuts.
- **Best for:** Brand films, big-idea or "why" lines, anything that should feel premium and timeless.

## Seal 3

**What it looks like:** Newspaper editorial collage with a giant serif masthead

- **Palette:** #0E0D0C, #F4F1EA, #E0322B, #7B4DFF, #EDE6D6
- **Type:** Giant high-contrast serif masthead word (400+ px) behind the subject; small bold sans caps captions.
- **Layout:** Masthead top, a black-and-white cut-out photo layered in front of its lower half, newspaper scans and scribbles behind.
- **Texture:** Halftone dots, newspaper column scans fading to black, heavy grain, deep vignette, hand-drawn red arrow and purple scribble lines.
- **Motion:** Masthead scales down into place, cut-out rises in front, arrow and scribbles draw on. Layered parallax, editorial pace.
- **Best for:** Hooks with a named subject, 'here's the real story' lines, news / trend explainers, authority beats.
