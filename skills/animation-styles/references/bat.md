# Bat family

Cinema eras: 1920s silent film, liminal found footage.

All styles here are coded (Remotion or HTML + ffmpeg). Plain-English look first, specs after.

## Bat 1

**What it looks like:** 1920s Silent Film: A baker's boy chases a runaway loaf downhill, then breaks it in half for a hungry girl

- **Palette:** #9D9995, #C4C2BF, #060302, #160B09, #0C0605
- **Type:** Playfair Display headline with Manrope body (Luxe italic pairing).
- **Layout:** Full-bleed type: Type fills the frame edge to edge; card tucks below.
- **Texture:** Film grain: Grain plus a soft vignette.
- **Motion:** Word slam: Words slam in from 125% with a small tilt.
- **Best for:** Comedy, Retro brands, Silent storytelling

## Bat 2

**What it looks like:** Liminal Found Footage: A new night-shift hire films their first night in an endless yellow office, following the rules on the wall

- **Palette:** #000000, #010000, #C4BF9E, #806A26, #89782C
- **Type:** Special Elite headline with Space Mono body (Typewriter pairing).
- **Layout:** Lower third: Card high, headline anchored bottom-left.
- **Texture:** CRT screen: Scanlines, phosphor glow, dark vignette.
- **Motion:** Mask wipe: Hard-edged wipes reveal each piece.
- **Best for:** Horror, Mystery & ARG, Game teasers
