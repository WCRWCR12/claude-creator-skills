# Raven family

Printmaking: woodcut, engraving, silkscreen and risograph print looks.

All styles here are coded (Remotion or HTML + ffmpeg). Plain-English look first, specs after.

## Raven 1

**What it looks like:** Woodcut Print: A village spends a whole winter casting one bell; the first time it rings, the snow stops

- **Palette:** #191918, #1D1D1B, #F2EBDB, #EBE4D4, #EEE7D7
- **Type:** Alfa Slab One headline with Oswald body (Slab pairing).
- **Layout:** Full-bleed type: Type fills the frame edge to edge; card tucks below.
- **Texture:** Ink & print: Hatched ink lines and print bleed on paper.
- **Motion:** Word slam: Words slam in from 125% with a small tilt.
- **Best for:** Folk tales, Craft brands, Book trailers

## Raven 2

**What it looks like:** Copperplate Engraving: A natural-history plate engraves itself: the burin cuts the copper, the bee builds up line by line, and a watercolour wash brings it to life

- **Palette:** #DED0AE, #DBCCAA, #111111, #715E45, #DED0AC
- **Type:** Fraunces headline with DM Sans body (Editorial serif pairing).
- **Layout:** Split: Headline owns the top half, the card the bottom.
- **Texture:** Ink & print: Hatched ink lines and print bleed on paper.
- **Motion:** Slide-stack: Lines rise and stack, staggered.
- **Best for:** Museum labels, Science explainers, Brand heritage

## Raven 3

**What it looks like:** Silkscreen Travel Poster: A traveller crosses the world one full-bleed screen print at a time, a ticket stub counting the distance from home, until the far side turns into the way back

- **Palette:** #0E1323, #171E48, #EADBB9, #BCA16B, #171E48
- **Type:** Anton headline with Inter body (Poster pairing).
- **Layout:** Full-bleed type: Type fills the frame edge to edge; card tucks below.
- **Texture:** Risograph: Off-register colour and speckle.
- **Motion:** Word slam: Words slam in from 125% with a small tilt.
- **Best for:** Destination guides, Tourism, Event posters

## Raven 4

**What it looks like:** Printed Long Take: Red-crowned cranes in a Hokkaido valley, one scratchboard take, counted back from 33 to 1,927

- **Palette:** #141519, #1C1C1F, #BEAA86, #706149, #86765B
- **Type:** Fraunces headline with DM Sans body (Editorial serif pairing).
- **Layout:** Tilted cards: Scrapbook: cards dropped at angles.
- **Texture:** Risograph: Off-register colour and speckle.
- **Motion:** Drop & bounce: Falls from above and bounces.
- **Best for:** Journeys, Wildlife, Landscapes
