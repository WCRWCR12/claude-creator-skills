# Fox family

Pixel / 16-bit game scenes.

All styles here are coded (Remotion or HTML + ffmpeg). Plain-English look first, specs after.

## Fox 1

**What it looks like:** 16-bit pixel village panel above the presenter

- **Palette:** #7EC8F2, #E4C9A0, #C8743C, #2A1F3D, #4AD991, #F08A5D
- **Type:** Pixel font (Press Start 2P) inside pixel UI panels; a mono caps caption tag in a black box at the seam.
- **Layout:** Top half: pixel scene (buildings, plaza, a crowd of sprites) with a pixel UI panel; presenter in the bottom half.
- **Texture:** Crisp pixel edges, banded sky, checker cobbles.
- **Motion:** Sprites drop in and bob on the beat; checklist ticks; progress bar fills.
- **Best for:** Simulations, 'it spins up 100 agents', playful explainers of a process.

## Fox 7

**What it looks like:** Pixel-Art Presenter: a Stardew-style game portrait of a presenter that pops up bottom-right to explain, talks in time with the voice-over, then pops away so the graphic gets the whole frame

- **Palette:** #2b1c14, #4a3122, #f0c6a3, #33241b, #6f8c80, #1d1d24, #e8b84a, #1d2333
- **Type:** No type of its own: it sits under whatever headline the reel's motion graphic uses.
- **Layout:** Head-and-shoulders game portrait (448 x 512 px) in the bottom-right corner on a small dark plate, like a Stardew Valley / SNES RPG dialogue box. Only on screen for some beats; when the presenter is off, the motion graphic fills the frame.
- **Texture:** Hand-placed pixel art on a 56 x 64 grid: bold dark outline, about 15 flat colours, 2-3 tones each, slightly big head. Dark-brown curls in clumps with highlights, short neat beard along a clear jaw, warm skin, small green-grey eyes under straight brows, black crew-neck tee, thin gold chain. Adapt hair, beard and clothes to your own character. Hands are real drawn hands: an open hand with four fanned fingers and a separate thumb, and a fist with knuckle bumps and a tucked thumb. Never a mitten or tube shape, never a single raised finger.
- **Motion:** Pops up from the bottom edge in 2-frame steps (overshoots to 110%, settles), about 7 frames, and pops away the same way. While on, the mouth opens and shuts with the voice-over (closed, small, open, wide), blinks at random and bobs one pixel while talking. Poses: talk, present (open palm held out with a small gold sparkle), think (fist under chin).
- **Best for:** Skill videos with no real footage: a host who narrates over the motion graphics, points at the thing being explained and gives a thumbs up on the payoff.

## Fox 8

**What it looks like:** Cosy Pixel-Art Presenter: a detailed cosy pixel-art character, cut out with no background, talking in sync with the voice-over and popping in for hook, explain and CTA lines

- **Palette:** #2b1c14, #5a3524, #e9a77f, #a5552e, #6f8c6a, #1d1a1c, #e8b84a
- **Type:** No type of its own: it sits under whatever headline and captions the reel uses.
- **Layout:** Head-and-shoulders bust standing on the bottom edge of the frame (no box, no plate, no background). Centre or bottom-right, any size; the bust is cut by the frame bottom.
- **Texture:** Detailed cosy pixel art with warm light (curly dark-brown hair with faded sides, short reddish-brown beard, green-grey eyes, black tee, gold chain - swap in your own character's traits). Cut out cleanly with a thin dark outline matching the art's own line work.
- **Motion:** Pops up from the bottom with a soft spring and drops away when the graphic needs the frame. While on: mouth shapes (lips shut, smile, teeth, half open, wide, round) follow the voice-over word by word; blinks; small nods on stressed words; a slow sway and breathing. Hand poses on cue: hands clasped (listening), open hands (explaining), excited hands (CTA, "free"), one palm held out (on "this", "called"), head tilt (questions). Gestures picked at random every 2-4 s while talking, never the same twice running: quick head turn, scratching head or beard, hand on chin thinking, finger guns, a point toward the content, a small shrug, a wave; blinks at irregular gaps with the odd double blink.
- **Best for:** Skill and explainer videos: the presenter pops in on hook, explaining and CTA lines over the scenes and pops out for graphic-only beats. Works with any voice-over plus its word timings.
