# Hawk family

Evidence films: satellite, LiDAR, teardowns, microscope, archive light table.

All styles here are coded (Remotion or HTML + ffmpeg). Plain-English look first, specs after.

## Hawk 1

**What it looks like:** Satellite Briefing: A satellite follows the map to the Aral Sea and finds a desert; only the part someone dammed came back

- **Palette:** #848683, #979B96, #F5F2EB, #292D2C, #979B96
- **Type:** Space Grotesk headline with Space Mono body (Tech grotesk pairing).
- **Layout:** Split: Headline owns the top half, the card the bottom.
- **Texture:** CRT screen: Scanlines, phosphor glow, dark vignette.
- **Motion:** Slide-stack: Lines rise and stack, staggered.
- **Best for:** Climate, News explainers, Geography
