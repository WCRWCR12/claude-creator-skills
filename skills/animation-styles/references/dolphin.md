# Dolphin family

Clean light product keynote: white/blue UI cards, italic-serif accent words.

All styles here are coded (Remotion or HTML + ffmpeg). Plain-English look first, specs after.

## Dolphin 1

**What it looks like:** Clean hook card: headline with a blue-boxed italic line over app UI

- **Palette:** #E9EDF2, #111111, #1F7BFF, #FFFFFF
- **Type:** Inter SemiBold headline line 1; line 2 in white italic serif (Instrument Serif) inside a solid blue box. Captions: sans with one italic-serif blue accent word.
- **Layout:** Top half: headline, then a real app/terminal window (and phone) floating; bottom half: face in a rounded-top panel.
- **Texture:** Blurred, brightened copy of the face footage as background; soft card shadows.
- **Motion:** Blue box scales out horizontally, cards pop in, captions run word by word with the italic accent word.
- **Best for:** Hooks for tool/product reels: 'Let X do Y', 'Remove X with Y'.

## Dolphin 2

**What it looks like:** Giant gradient counter on a frosted glass card

- **Palette:** #0F1216, #E9F3FF, #5AA9FF, rgba(255,255,255,.1)
- **Type:** Inter ExtraBold 250 px number with a blue-to-white gradient, small grey label under it.
- **Layout:** Number top-centre, frosted card of supporting bars or a screenshot below, face panel at the bottom.
- **Texture:** Dark blurred background, glass card, soft glow.
- **Motion:** Number counts up with ease-out; bars fill one by one; card pops in.
- **Best for:** Any stat: hours saved, success rate, stars, users, money.

## Dolphin 3

**What it looks like:** Numbered tool reveal: 'Tool Number One' over a browser card

- **Palette:** #FFFFFF, #111111, #1F7BFF
- **Type:** 'Tool Number' in Inter SemiBold, the number word in white italic serif on a blue box; product name big below.
- **Layout:** Full white frame: label top, a browser window with a blue outline in the middle, name and a one-line description under it.
- **Texture:** Pure white, real screenshot inside the browser frame.
- **Motion:** Number box pops, browser card scales in and the page scrolls slowly inside it.
- **Best for:** Listicles of tools/features ('3 tools that...'), product tours.

## Dolphin 4

**What it looks like:** Editorial cream grid: giant two-tone headline in a floating card

- **Palette:** #F4F1EA, #FFFDF8, #141414, #E8553E, #3A3631
- **Type:** Archivo Black 200+ px headline, black word over coral word, cropped by the card edge; coral italic serif kicker; mono pill tags.
- **Layout:** Grid-paper top half with a floating card; dark mono caption tag at the seam; presenter in a warm studio below.
- **Texture:** Fine grid paper, deep soft card shadow.
- **Motion:** Card rises and straightens; the second headline line slides in from the right; tags type on.
- **Best for:** Naming a product or offer ('Meet [Product]'), library or resource reveals, before/after lines.

## Dolphin 5

**What it looks like:** Deck-slide explainer: step counter, two-tone headline, app window

- **Palette:** #EEF2F6, #111111, #1F7BFF, #22C55E
- **Type:** Inter Bold headline, line 1 black, line 2 blue; mono step counter '$ 03/15' and breadcrumb.
- **Layout:** Left-aligned slide: counter top-left, headline, a wide app/terminal window, presenter bottom.
- **Texture:** Flat pale blue-grey, real window chrome.
- **Motion:** Headline lines rise in, terminal lines tick in with green checks, progress dots fill.
- **Best for:** Step-by-step setup tutorials (10+ steps).

## Dolphin 6

**What it looks like:** Floating phone UI beside the talking head

- **Palette:** #1F3D2B, #F2EDE3, #9BE3A8, #FFFFFF
- **Type:** Single white serif word captions at chest height; the app UI uses a display serif plus Inter.
- **Layout:** Face fills the frame; an iPhone with the real app screen floats in the top-right corner.
- **Texture:** Real footage, phone with a soft shadow.
- **Motion:** Phone drops in and straightens; app screens change in sync with the words; buttons press.
- **Best for:** Product demos while talking; app feature walk-throughs.

## Dolphin 7

**What it looks like:** Off-white split: giant app icon over an italic serif keyword

- **Palette:** #F5F3EF, #141414, #F26B3A, #FF3B30
- **Type:** Italic condensed serif caps keyword (1-3 words), black; small orange corner brackets.
- **Layout:** Off-white top half with one huge real app icon or object; face in a rounded-top panel below.
- **Texture:** Flat warm off-white, soft icon shadow.
- **Motion:** Icon pops and un-rotates, brackets snap on, keyword rises in; the icon swaps on each named app.
- **Best for:** Naming platforms/apps ('LinkedIn, Instagram...'), keyword-per-line body sections.
