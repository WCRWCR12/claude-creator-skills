# Reel Kit family

Showcase reels where every spoken line fires a different named full-frame effect over a locked talking head.

All styles here are coded (Remotion or HTML + ffmpeg). Plain-English look first, specs after.

## Reel Kit

**What it looks like:** Locked talking head with a pinned effects list; every line cuts to a different full-frame effect (tracking box, dial, orbiting type, letter dive, banknote, ink impact)

- **Palette:** #1F3A2B, #2F5D45, #EFE8D8, #111111, #C9B458, #FFFFFF
- **Type:** DM Serif Display for display words and numerals, Inter ExtraBold for UI and caption pills, Archivo Black for the impact word, JetBrains Mono for the list header. Captions are white rounded pills with green text.
- **Layout:** Head fills the 9:16 frame, eyes upper third; a dark-green rounded list panel pinned right (active row white, gold more-button); full-frame effects on cream paper or dark green hide the list; headline pill top-left on frame zero.
- **Texture:** Flat colour. Cream paper sheets lift with a soft shadow; one-bit ink threshold on the impact frame; engraved concentric line work on the banknote. No gradients.
- **Motion:** One effect per ~1.1-1.5 s, hard cut in and out, back to the head between; the list row follows the cut; internal motion per effect (box snaps on, dial turns, letters orbit, camera dives through a letter, letters drop into a comment box, layers fan out in 3D, counter rolls, speed lines burst).
- **Best for:** Capability showcases and 'what my Claude can do' reels, product-as-proof edits, list-style reels with a comment-keyword CTA.
