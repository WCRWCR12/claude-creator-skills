# Owl family

Explainer diagrams: wireframes, charts, metaphors drawn out.

All styles here are coded (Remotion or HTML + ffmpeg). Plain-English look first, specs after.

## Owl 1

**What it looks like:** Cream wireframe explainer: thick black outlines, yellow tags

- **Palette:** #EFE8D8, #121212, #FFD23F, #3E9B5B, #FBF7EE
- **Type:** Archivo Black caps headings with a yellow numbered box; bold sans UI text; caption pill with one yellow-highlighted word.
- **Layout:** Level stepper top, step title, a phone/card wireframe centre, caption pill bottom.
- **Texture:** Flat cream, 5-7 px black outlines, hard offset shadows (neo-brutalist).
- **Motion:** Bubbles and cards scale from their corner; the stepper advances per level.
- **Best for:** Funnels, levels/stages, longer explainers (60-120 s).

## Owl 2

**What it looks like:** Data-journalism orange: mono source tags, bars, big number flip

- **Palette:** #FAFAF8, #141414, #FF5A1F, #F7A27F, #8A8A8A
- **Type:** Inter ExtraBold headline with an orange second line; JetBrains Mono tags ('02 / 05', 'SOURCE ...'); a huge number.
- **Layout:** Grid-paper white top: tags, headline, bar chart, big number; presenter strip at the bottom.
- **Texture:** Fine grid, flat orange bars.
- **Motion:** Bars grow in sequence, numbers count; the big number flips from before to after.
- **Best for:** Research-backed claims, comparisons, 'where your time goes'.

## Owl 3

**What it looks like:** Metaphor diagram: fishing line arcs over the water

- **Palette:** #F3EADB, #3FB6C8, #1D6F8C, #D63A2F, #FFD23F
- **Type:** Inter ExtraBold three-word title; mono labels on the diagram.
- **Layout:** Cream top with title, a sea band across the middle, the rod line arcing to the hook; presenter strip below.
- **Texture:** Flat illustration with a gradient sea and an animated wave line.
- **Motion:** The line draws along the arc, the hook rides the tip, labels appear at each phase.
- **Best for:** Abstract concepts with a physical metaphor (hooks, funnels, pipes).

## Owl 4

**What it looks like:** Countdown checklist overlay with live stat cards

- **Palette:** #FFFFFF, #FFD23F, #E5322D, #111111
- **Type:** White title box, red mono countdown timer, numbered list in bold sans, covered items highlighted yellow.
- **Layout:** Face full-frame; title top, 1-10 list down the left, stat card lower third, comment CTA footer.
- **Texture:** Real footage, flat white cards.
- **Motion:** Timer counts down; each item highlights as it is covered; a stat card pops for each.
- **Best for:** Top-10 / top-20 lists, rapid-fire tips.

## Owl 5

**What it looks like:** Cream & coral · Playful · Centred card · Brutalist · Spring pop

- **Palette:** #F4F1EA, #141414, #E8553E, #F6C9BE, #FFFDF8
- **Type:** Titan One headline with Fredoka body (Playful pairing).
- **Layout:** Centred card: Headline up top, one UI card centre stage.
- **Texture:** Brutalist: Thick outlines, hard offset shadows.

## Owl 6
