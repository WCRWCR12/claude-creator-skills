# Mockup Showcase family

Prop worlds: one tactile 3D scene per sentence.

All styles here are coded (Remotion or HTML + ffmpeg). Plain-English look first, specs after.

## Mockup Showcase

**What it looks like:** Prop worlds: one tactile scene per sentence (tablet, ripple stripes, retro Mac, island, CRT), hard cuts, springy overshoot, 3D type

- **Palette:** One dominant flat colour per world: paper grey, red and cream, sky blue, teal, green mat, dark brown; beige retro computer; pink slam tag; yellow handwriting
- **Type:** Heavy rounded caps in light grey with hard darker extrusion and soft shadow, typed letter by letter; black block caps for the big word; marker handwriting
- **Layout:** One centred prop or sticker figure per world with 3D title above; props with real screens inside
- **Texture:** Paper grain, dark vignette, soft drop shadows, perforated stamp edge, grid mat
- **Motion:** Hard cuts every 1.1-2.5 s, spring overshoot entrances, slow camera push, ripple stripes, tag slam, shrink-to-speck, pull-back into a TV screen
- **Best for:** Personal-story or journey reels where each line gets a new visual world; showing real product screens inside props
