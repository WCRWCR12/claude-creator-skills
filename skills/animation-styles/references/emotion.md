# Emotion family

Editorial explainers driven by the edit: transitions and pace carry the feeling.

All styles here are coded (Remotion or HTML + ffmpeg). Plain-English look first, specs after.

## Emotion

**What it looks like:** White editorial explainer with mustard accent, B/W photo slots and ring / whip transitions

- **Palette:** #FDFDFD, #111111, #C09700, #E9D78F, #6E6E6E
- **Type:** Lines type on letter by letter mixing bold sans (Inter ExtraBold), italic serif (Playfair) and heavy condensed caps (Anton); mustard highlight boxes sweep behind key words.
- **Layout:** Bright white stage; one idea per scene; photo slots as round B/W avatars, a hero portrait on a mustard disc, white UI cards with soft shadows; mustard doodles (dotted paths, 4-point sparkles, pixel-dot clusters, corner brackets).
- **Texture:** Clean, flat white; soft drop shadows; motion blur only in transitions.
- **Motion:** ~1.5-2.5 s scenes, beat word-swaps, constant slow camera push/rotate; transitions: ring burst, zoom-through, motion-blur whip, black/inverted flash frames on drops, zoom-out to a speck, mustard band wipe that becomes a rail, swipe-up, highlight sweep; quiet near-silent tail.
- **Best for:** Punchy idea or lesson videos, product turns ('then meet [Product]'), anything that needs tension then release; animation-first showcases.
