## Skill Reel (Something 1)

**What it looks like:** Skill reel: isometric blue-block graphic on a black stage with a small pixel-art presenter in the corner

- **Palette:** #08090B, #BFE2FF, #4A97E8, #2C6CC0, #FFD07A, #EFEFEF
- **Type:** Inter Bold caption under the scene, Space Mono labels on the chips; headline word highlighted on the hook/CTA beats.
- **Layout:** Black 9:16 stage. Top half: a row of glossy isometric blue blocks (one per item), a headline strip, and a glow on the active block. Bottom-right corner: a small pixel-art presenter on hook and CTA beats; graphic only on the explain beats.
- **Texture:** Flat colour. Glossy gradient tops on the blocks, soft blue radial glow on black, no scene gradients, no decorative micro-text.
- **Motion:** Blocks drop in one by one on the beat; the key block lifts and changes colour; the presenter pops in on the hook and the CTA and stays off-frame on explain lines. Hard cuts between scenes, about 1.5 s per beat.
- **Best for:** Skill showcases: name a Claude skill and show what it does with named items plus one highlight beat, with the presenter carrying the hook and the call to action.
