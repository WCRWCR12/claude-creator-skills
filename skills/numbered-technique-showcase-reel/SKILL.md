# Numbered technique showcase reel

Technique inspired by: @science_in_8bit (Instagram)

A high-performing reel format for demonstrating a set of related skills. The structure turns an abstract skill list into a visual proof of concept. This skill describes a filming/animation structure only.

## The structure

1. **Split-screen hook.** Two versions of the same scene side by side: "without the skill" (label: NO CAMERA) vs "with the skill" (label: REC · CAMERA). No words needed; the difference is the hook.
2. **Numbered walk-through.** Three techniques, each with:
   - A bold typographic label (e.g. "01 / 03 - PULL BACK")
   - A short italic subtitle that sets the scene ("the harbour was all around")
   - The technique playing out in full, no cut-away, let it breathe
3. **Payoff line.** One stark statement in large white caps ("WE SHOOT OUR FILMS LIKE THIS"). It switches from demonstration to identity claim.
4. **Social proof moment.** Big bold numbers (views, followers) on the same illustrated background. Visual weight matches the claim.
5. **Summary card.** A small split layout showing all three techniques as thumbnails with labels (01 PULL BACK / 02 DOLLY ZOOM / 03 CRANE + LAND). It makes the system feel deliberate and teachable.
6. **Comment-keyword CTA.** "Want it? Comment STUDIO and we'll send you the link." Overlaid on the original split-screen, closing the loop visually.

## Visual style

- Flat-colour illustrated scenes throughout: no live footage, no real photography
- Palette: deep teal sky, warm amber sunset, dark slate buildings, cream/white type
- Typography: bold uppercase sans for technique names, italic serif for subtitles, both on the illustration rather than on a card
- Corner bracket marks (like a camera viewfinder) frame key scenes
- A REC indicator dot labels the "with-camera" split

## What makes it work

- The side-by-side before/after is the entire hook: no talking head, no intro
- Each technique is numbered (01/03) so the viewer knows the shape of the reel before it ends
- The illustration style lets the motion be the star, with no distracting real-world noise
- Social proof comes after the demonstration, so it is earned rather than claimed
- The comment-keyword CTA is specific ("STUDIO") and promise-based ("we'll send you the link")

## Adapting it to your own topic

Replace the cinematic moves with any numbered skill set:
- "Three things your Claude can do that mine can't": show each one live
- "Three ways we cut a client's workload in half": a before/after split per workflow
- Swap the illustration for Remotion-coded motion graphics in the same flat-colour palette
- Keep the numbered label system (01 / 03 bold caps plus italic subtitle)
- Keep the split-screen hook as the opening
- Adapt the payoff line: "WE BUILD SYSTEMS LIKE THIS"
- Adapt the CTA keyword to your own offer

## Notes

- The reel runs about 30 s. Three techniques is the ceiling for this format; more loses the split-screen payoff.
- Do not add a host talking head. The technique is the presenter.
- Keep music at 5% or under; the visuals carry the weight.
