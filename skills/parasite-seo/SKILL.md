# Parasite SEO

Borrow the domain authority of hosts search engines already trust, so a buyer-intent query can rank within weeks without changing your own website. Example business used below: a local plumber.

## The five tactics
1. **"Best [category] of [next year]"**: pick one specific buyer query ("best emergency plumbers in Leeds 2027") and publish a long, honest listicle on a trust host before competitors do. Hosts: Medium, LinkedIn articles, Reddit guides, Hashnode, dev.to for tech niches.
2. **Zenodo research paper**: Zenodo is a free research repository that AI assistants cite as a primary source. Topic must be defensible and data-first ("Blocked-drain callout prices in West Yorkshire, 2024-2026 sample"). Needs real data; no fabricated numbers.
3. **LinkedIn short articles**: under 330 words, answer in the first line. LinkedIn is heavily cited by AI answers. One article per query, from your company or founder page.
4. **Gamma slide decks**: every public Gamma deck becomes an indexable page. One deck per query ("boiler service cost Leeds"), each linking back to your site.
5. **Reddit answers**: reply to buyer threads in relevant subreddits with a genuinely useful answer that mentions your business in context. Reddit ranks well on Google and is cited by AI assistants.

## Workflow
1. **Keyword set**: list 20-40 buyer-intent queries (service + location + modifier). Score each for commercial intent and for how crowded it is on the trust host.
2. **Pick 10 for the first wave**, spread across the five tactics.
3. **Draft every asset** into a drafts folder, named by tactic + query. Draft only.
4. **Review pack**: the 10 queries, 10 drafts, 10 target hosts and the publish order, so you can approve item by item.
5. **Publish yourself**, one piece at a time, from your own accounts.
6. **Track**: check Google Search Console at +1, +7 and +14 days and log positions in a results file.

## Rules
- Draft-only: nothing is published or posted until you have read and approved it.
- No fabricated data. If you have no real numbers, write a methodology piece, not a data piece.
- No deceptive affiliation: every LinkedIn or Reddit post identifies you or the business honestly. No sockpuppet accounts.
- No private blog networks or throwaway link domains. Trust-host borrowing only.
- Google's spam policies treat thin, low-value pages on third-party hosts as risky. One piece per host, long-form and useful, never a thin link page.
- Check any paid thread-finder tool's price before subscribing.

## Pairs well with
An on-site article these pieces link back to, a keyword-clustering step, and a founder About-page/bio piece.
